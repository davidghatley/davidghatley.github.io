01Construction

How it is built

Pip 57's cells drawn 150 units wide and 100 tall. Wherever two cells touch only at a corner, the triangle between them is filled, so diagonals run at a clean slope instead of stepping.

A 40-unit slit is cut through the middle of every horizontal bar three or more cells long, which reads as a stencil and keeps the wide bars from going flat.

A vector face: it works at any size. The tester and the waterfall use the house steps.
