01Construction

How it is built

Capitals are three cells wide and five tall. M, N and W take four or five cells, because a three-cell M is a guess.

It is unicase: lowercase sets the capitals, so a label stays one height. At 16 px every cell is two pixels and nothing is grey.

One cell is the font size divided by eight, so at 16, 32, 48, 72 and 120 px every edge lands on a whole pixel. The tester and the waterfall only offer those sizes.
