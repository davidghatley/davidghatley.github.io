01Construction

How it is built

Every cell is its own round dot, 0.84 of a cell across, so dots never touch and letters read as lamps on a board.

Most 5×7 faces skip lowercase. Pip 57 has a five-row x-height and two-row descenders, so g, j, p, q and y hang below the line. It is monospaced on a six-cell advance.

One cell is the font size divided by eight, so at 16, 32, 48, 72 and 120 px every edge lands on a whole pixel. The tester and the waterfall only offer those sizes.
