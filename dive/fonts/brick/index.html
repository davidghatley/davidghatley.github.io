01Construction

How it is built

Hoop's skeletons with every arc replaced by its 45-degree points, so bowls become octagons, at 74% width under a 132-unit square pen.

A stroke end that meets nothing gets a square cap; where strokes meet, the outside of the turn is filled with a bevel, so nothing pokes out of a joint. Each glyph is then cut flat at the lines its skeleton reaches, so diagonals stand on the baseline instead of hanging below it.

A vector face: it works at any size. The tester and the waterfall use the house steps.
