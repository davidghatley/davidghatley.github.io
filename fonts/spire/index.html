01Construction

How it is built

Hoop's skeletons with every arc pushed toward a superellipse (exponent 0.3), so bowls get straight sides, then squeezed to half width and stroked at 132 units with round ends.

Counters close to slots, which is the point: it fills a tall poster column edge to edge.

A vector face: it works at any size. The tester offers the house steps.
