01Construction

How it is built

Each Hoop Bold glyph is cut down its middle. The left half is redrawn as grid cells, half a stroke wide, wherever the cell's centre falls inside the letter; the right half keeps the vector outline.

The seam is the idea: every letter shows the bitmap it came from and the curve it became.

A vector face: it works at any size. The tester offers the house steps.
