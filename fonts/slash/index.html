01Construction

How it is built

Hoop's skeletons with squared bowls, sheared 14 degrees and stroked at 170 units with flat ends.

Then a set of parallel cuts, 30 units wide and 330 apart, rising at 34 degrees, is subtracted from every glyph. The cuts line up from letter to letter, so a word reads as one ribbon.

A vector face: it works at any size. The tester offers the house steps.
