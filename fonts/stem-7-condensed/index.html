01Construction

How it is built

Every run of identical columns in Stem 7 Regular collapses to one, so O narrows from five cells to three and M from seven to five while stems and bars keep their weight.

Collapsing breaks some things a rule can't fix, so they are drawn by hand: all ten figures sit on one 4-cell box so columns of numbers line up, the dashes step 2, 3 and 5 cells, and l and [ are kept apart from L and C.

One cell is the font size divided by eight, so at 16, 32, 48, 72 and 120 px every edge lands on a whole pixel. The tester and the waterfall only offer those sizes.
