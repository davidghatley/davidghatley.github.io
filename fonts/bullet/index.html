01Construction

How it is built

Every capital, figure and mark of Hoop Bold is scaled to 62% and subtracted from a 720-unit disc. Lowercase types the capitals, so a route name can't come out wrong.

A vector face: it works at any size. The tester and the waterfall use the house steps.
