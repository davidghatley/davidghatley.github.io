01Construction

How it is built

Every column of Stem 7 Regular is doubled, so a one-cell stem becomes two cells while bars stay one cell tall. The side bearings and the space double too, so letters keep Regular's stem-to-gap rhythm instead of crowding.

Regular's kerning is doubled to match, and the one-cell dh ligature is switched off, the way Bold does it, because a thin mark among wide letters reads as a mistake.

One cell is the font size divided by eight, so at 16, 32, 48, 72 and 120 px every edge lands on a whole pixel. The tester and the waterfall only offer those sizes.
