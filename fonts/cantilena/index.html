01Construction

How it is built

Bowls are cubic superellipses, an outer curve minus an inner one, so the thick sides and the hairline tops and bottoms are exact. Serifs are thin slabs joined to the stem by circular brackets; arm ends taper into beaks with a concave inner edge; curve ends hang a ball terminal off a hairline neck.

Shapes that a broad pen would write, like S and the 2, are traced by stamping a flat nib along a skeleton, then refitted as smooth cubics. Full upper and lowercase, figures and the Spanish accents, with capitals and lowercase kerned separately.

A vector face: it works at any size. The tester and the waterfall use the house steps.
