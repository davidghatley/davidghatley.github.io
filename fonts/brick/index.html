01Construction

How it is built

The same octagonal skeletons as Quire, at 62% width, stroked with a 168-unit square pen. Joins are mitred and ends are square, so every bowl turns in hard 45-degree facets.

Where Quire is written, Brick is built: the same letters, laid like masonry.

A vector face: it works at any size. The tester offers the house steps.
